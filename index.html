I work with ISVs and technology companies to build, modernise, and scale software products — with engineering teams that ship outcomes, not just code. As EVP of Product Engineering Services at Cybage Software, I partner with technology-enabled enterprises across payments, energy, cybersecurity, and SaaS platforms to solve the hard problems: legacy modernisation that doesn't break what works, cloud architecture that actually reduces operational risk, and AI integration that improves engineering velocity without sacrificing quality or accountability.
